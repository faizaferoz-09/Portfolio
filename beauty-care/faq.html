Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury beauty cosmetics, skincare formulations, and shipping.

Home FAQ
Orders & Shipping
What is the shipping cost and timeline?
We offer free delivery for orders above $150. For smaller orders, shipping is flat $15. Delivery within Malaysia takes 2-4 business days, while international shipping to Singapore and Indonesia takes 4-7 business days.
Can I track my order once placed?
Yes! Once shipped, you will receive an email containing a custom tracking number and URL link to check transit stages in real-time.
Ingredients & Formulations
Are SIMPLON Beauty products cruelty-free?
Absolutely. We are certified cruelty-free. None of our final formulations or raw ingredients undergo animal testing at any phase of research and manufacturing.
Are the products safe for highly sensitive skin?
All our skincare products are clinically tested and dermatologist-approved. If you have extreme skin sensitivities, we recommend performing a patch test on a small area of skin before applying it fully to your face.
Do your shampoos contain sulfates or parabens?
No. Our entire hair care range (including the best-selling Keratin Repair series) is 100% free of silicones, sulfates, parabens, and harsh chemical fillers.
Secure Payments & Returns
What payment options do you support?
We support Visa, Mastercard, and FPX Online Banking. All inputs are secured by 256-bit encryption through our secure checkout portal.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return window. If you're not satisfied, return the product in its original packaging for a full refund or exchange. Contact support for instructions.
